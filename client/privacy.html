Transparency & Ethics

Privacy Policy & Ethical AI Principles

Our commitment to candidate privacy, synthetic dataset transparency, and responsible machine learning.

⚖️ Educational & Guidance Disclaimer

The predictions generated by CareerPredict AI are calculated using a statistical Logistic Regression binary classification model. These calculations are strictly intended for educational self-assessment, skill prioritization, and career guidance purposes. They do not constitute a legal, formal, or binding guarantee of employment or hiring by any specific company or employer.

📊 Synthetic Dataset Disclosure

To ensure strict candidate privacy and eliminate personal data harvesting, the model weights were trained and evaluated on a mathematically generated synthetic dataset (5,000 samples) with calibrated multivariate distributions reflecting industry placement correlations.

No proprietary applicant resumes, confidential employer hiring databases, or private candidate records were scraped or ingested during the training pipeline.

🛡️ Ethical AI & Bias Mitigation

  • Explainable Decisions: Unlike opaque black-box neural networks, Logistic Regression provides fully auditable linear coefficients for all input features.
  • Non-Discriminatory Feature Weights: Demographic attributes (gender, age) are intentionally assigned zero or near-zero statistical weights in the decision logic to ensure meritocratic evaluation focused on technical capability and problem solving.
  • Data Ownership: Candidates maintain full control over their account data and can permanently purge all stored assessment snapshots at any time via the Profile settings.

🔒 Data Protection & Storage

Authentication tokens are verified using Supabase Auth standards. Candidate inputs and prediction histories are securely isolated per user account. We do not sell, rent, or distribute candidate assessment data to third-party recruiters or advertising networks.
